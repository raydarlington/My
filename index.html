Darlington Raymond

Darlington Raymond is a visual artist whose figurative practice explores people, human emotion and lived experience. Through portraiture, gesture and expression, his work reflects on vulnerability, resilience and the experiences people carry with them.

Darlington Raymond works primarily with soft pastels and acrylic. His paintings are rooted in people and lived experience, using the human figure to explore vulnerability, resilience and emotion.

His work was recently featured in the Paisley Daily Express.

Darlington holds a B.Tech in Estate Management and an MSc in Project Management. He is currently a member of Visual Arts Scotland and the Aberdeen Artists Society.

Darlington Raymond

My practice is centred on people, emotions and lived experience. I am curious about the moments that reveal what people may be experiencing in every way of existing. I want the work to hold attention beyond the surface level and create a connection between the viewer and the subject.
